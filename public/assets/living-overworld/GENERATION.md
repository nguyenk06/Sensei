# Living Overworld seasonal generation record

The accepted autumn and winter release uses separate original mobile and desktop landscape illustrations generated specifically for Sensei.

## Autumn proof

Direction: a mobile-first Minecraft-inspired voxel/pixel-art autumn valley. The mobile master contains a complete timber retreat, foreground path and grass, a small stream, dense red/orange/gold trees, distant mountains, and open sky. Its desktop crop master preserves the same geography at a smaller optical scale so Sensei's shallow banner retains the house, valley, foreground, and celestial corridor. Exclusions: sun, moon, weather, creatures, text, logos, and watermarks.

## Winter proof

Direction: a distinct mobile-first Minecraft-inspired winter woodland. The mobile master contains a complete warm timber retreat, snowy foreground, dense varied evergreen forest, wooded mountain depth, a small frozen stream, and open sky. Its desktop crop master preserves the same geography at a smaller optical scale for the shallow banner. Exclusions: sun, moon, stars, active snowfall, weather, creatures, text, logos, and watermarks.

The mobile masters were generated from text only. Desktop composition proofs were expanded from their corresponding mobile masters, and the runtime desktop crop masters were adapted from the approved desktop compositions without changing the scene identity. Spring/summer generation and asset-format optimization require later approval gates.
