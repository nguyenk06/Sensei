Both PNGs have transparent outer corners and are composed independently from the existing waterfall, lake, house, mountains, and environmental objects.

## Artwork method

The two assets were generated as original raster artwork with the approved existing Living Overworld assets supplied as visual references. They were then inspected at actual proof scale and integrated only into the isolated HTML composition.

Final hillside direction: create a transparent modular terrain connector in the established detailed voxel/pixel-hybrid autumn style; begin as a low meadow on the left, rise through multiple distinct rocky ledges and elevations, and meet the waterfall formation on the right; preserve consistent geology, warm directional light, and perspective; include no water, trees, house, mountains, sky, or background.

Final forest direction: create a wide transparent distant forest silhouette in the established Living Overworld autumn style; use varied conifers and deciduous trees with atmospheric perspective, deepest on the left and tapering to the right; include no mountains, terrain, sky, glow, or background.

A combined grove experiment was rejected because its semi-transparent color halo would have produced a visible rectangular seam. The proof instead reuses the clean individual modular trees, bushes, grass, flowers, and rocks with deterministic authored placement.

## Integration boundary

These files are candidates for later Product Owner-approved runtime integration. The proof does not alter production scene metadata, assets, weather, creature behavior, or application code.
