# Living Overworld Winter Composition Proof

Status: **Product Owner accepted and integrated into Living Overworld.**

This is a fresh mobile-first winter composition proof. It does not reuse the fall geography, and it does not change the Sensei runtime.

## Direction represented

- Soft illustrative Minecraft-inspired voxel/pixel artwork.
- Dense snow-covered evergreen woodland as the primary winter identity.
- A complete warm retreat on the right, more visually prominent than the fall retreat but still secondary to the landscape.
- A narrow frozen stream through the center rather than a dominant lake.
- Complete foreground, middle ground, mountains, forest, house, and transparent sky on mobile.
- A centered 20–25% open-sky corridor for an independent sun or moon.
- Mobile master first; desktop expands the same world laterally without mirroring or changing landmark order.
- Neutral daytime base only. Lighting, celestial bodies, clouds, weather, creatures, and foreground effects remain separate and deferred.
